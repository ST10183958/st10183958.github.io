MY JOURNEY

Professional Reflection

Reflecting on what I have learned and how I plan to develop my future career in Information Technology.

01

What Have I Learned?

During my education, I have developed a broader understanding of Information Technology and the different areas involved in software development.

I have learned how programming concepts can be applied to create practical applications and how different technologies can work together as part of a larger system.

02

My Strongest IT Skill

One of my strongest skills is programming and logical problem solving. Developing software requires breaking larger problems into smaller, manageable components.

Working on projects has helped me become more comfortable with analysing problems and developing solutions.

03

A Skill I Want to Improve

One area I would like to improve is my ability to design polished and user-friendly interfaces.

Improving my front-end development and design skills will allow me to create applications that are not only functional but also easier and more enjoyable for users to interact with.

04

How Will I Develop This Skill?

I plan to improve this skill through continued practice, personal projects, online learning resources, and by studying how professional applications are designed.

Building projects will allow me to experiment with different layouts and approaches while gaining practical experience.

05

How This Portfolio Supports My Future

This portfolio provides a place where I can demonstrate the knowledge and skills I have developed throughout my education.

It can also be updated as I gain new experience, complete additional projects, develop new technical skills, and progress towards my future career in Information Technology.
